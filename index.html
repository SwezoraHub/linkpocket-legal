LinkPocket — Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 2026 | Powered by Swezora

1. Privacy Policy

No Account or Login: LinkPocket works without requiring user accounts or login.

Local Device Storage: All links, titles, categories, archives, and theme preferences you save are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your saved links to external servers.

Purchases & Restore: Optional one-time lifetime upgrades to unlock unlimited links are processed through Google Play Billing. Purchase status and restore functionality rely on your Google Play store account.

2. Terms & Conditions

LinkPocket is provided as an offline-first link saving utility. You are solely responsible for the links you save, archive, or open using external apps or browsers.
